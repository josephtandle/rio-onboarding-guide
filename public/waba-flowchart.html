A dedicated phone number not currently on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app

Q3: Creating a New WABA or Using an Existing One?
WABA = WhatsApp Business Account
Enter Business Info
Business name, website, category, timezone
Select Existing WABA
Choose from your existing WhatsApp Business Accounts in the dropdown
Add Phone Number + Display Name
Enter the number you want to use for WhatsApp Business. Choose SMS or call verification.
⛔ ERROR: Number Already Has a BSP Partner
Locked to another WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (ManyChat, Wati, etc.)
Fix: Disconnect from Current BSP
WhatsApp Business Manager → Phone Numbers → Release Number. Wait 24–48 hours for propagation.
⛔ ERROR: Number Registered to Existing WhatsApp Account
Number is still active in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app
Fix: Release Number in Meta Business Manager
Go to WhatsApp Accounts → select your WABA → Phone Numbers → release the number. Open Meta Business Manager → Then wait up to 3 minutes and retry.
⛔ OTP Not Received
Verification code never arrived via SMS
Fix: Request New Code or Switch to Call
Wait 60 seconds, then tap "Call me instead"
Verify Phone Number (Enter OTP)
Enter the 6-digit code sent via SMS or voice call to confirm ownership
Review: What You're Sharing with Rio
Rio will be able to manage WhatsApp accounts, access conversations, and send messages on your behalf
Connecting Your Account...
Rio is finalizing the connection with Meta. This may take a few moments.
✓ Your Account is Connected to Rio
WABA linked successfully. Rio will review your business within 24 hours — you'll only be contacted if there's an issue.
